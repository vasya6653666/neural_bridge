# Figr for NeuralBridge

Figr is an AI product design tool that takes teams from idea to shippable design through exploration, collaboration and iteration — grounded in their existing product and design system.

This Power connects NeuralBridge to Figr over MCP. Create and edit canvases, design systems, and org skills from NeuralBridge, or port screens into your repo.

## Install

1. Install the power from the **Powers** panel in NeuralBridge IDE.
2. Sign in with your Figr account when NeuralBridge prompts

Requires NeuralBridge.
